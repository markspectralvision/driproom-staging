General questions

What is IV therapy?

Intravenous therapy delivers fluids, vitamins and minerals directly into a vein through a small catheter. Because the nutrients enter your bloodstream, they bypass the digestive tract completely and become available to your cells right away.

How long is a visit?

Plan for about 45 minutes in the lounge. Your provider will talk through your goals before the drip begins.

Who provides the drips?

Licensed providers deliver the drips, with medical oversight by Parie Med Spa.

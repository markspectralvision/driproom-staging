Parie Med Spa has spent years on the aesthetic side of looking well. Somewhere in that work a pattern showed up. The guests who got the best results were the ones who felt well underneath. Hydrated. Rested. Energised. So we built a room for that part, staffed it with the same licensed providers, kept the same medical oversight, and gave it a simpler promise. Wellness first.

Where science meets wellness.

The Parie Med Spa philosophy.

Who will be looking after you?

Every drip at The Drip Room is placed by a licensed clinician, under the same medical oversight that runs Parie Med Spa. Not a technician. Not a trainee. Between them, the team below has spent decades in critical care, hospital nursing and aesthetic medicine, and the reason that matters is simple. An IV is a clinical procedure, and you should be able to see exactly who is holding the needle.

You are in safe hands

Your provider walks you through what is in the bag before it goes in, why each ingredient is there, and what to expect while it runs. Ask anything. Ask twice. Nobody here is in a hurry, and nobody will talk over your head. Educating you is part of the appointment, not an extra.

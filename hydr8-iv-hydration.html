Why make time for Hydr8 IV?

Dehydration rarely announces itself. It shows up as a dull afternoon, a headache you blame on screens, muscles that cramp on the second mile. By the time you feel thirsty you are already behind. Las Vegas makes it worse. Dry desert air pulls moisture out of you all day, indoors and out.
